FAQ

Questions learners ask

What is the 3-for-1 logbook scheme?

In NSW, every 1 hour of driving with a licensed driving instructor can be recorded as 3 hours in your learner logbook, for up to 10 instructor hours (30 logbook hours). Bonus hours count as daytime driving. Digital logbook apps add the bonus automatically.

What is the Safer Drivers Course?

A NSW course for learners under 25 who have at least 50 logbook hours. Finishing it adds 20 hours to your logbook. It's run by approved providers – we'll get you ready for it and can point you to the nearest course in Newcastle.

How many hours do I need before my test?

Learners under 25 need 120 supervised hours, including 20 hours at night. Combining 10 instructor lessons (30 hrs) with the Safer Drivers Course (20 hrs) takes 50 hours off that total.

Can I use your car for my driving test?

Yes. Our test-day package includes pick-up, a 1-hour warm-up on local test routes, use of the dual-control car for the test, and drop-off afterwards.

Do you teach manual?

No – we teach in an automatic car only, which is what most learners now choose.

Do you help overseas licence holders?

Yes. We offer refresher lessons and NSW road-rule coaching in English, Hindi or Punjabi.

What if I need to cancel?

Please give at least 24 hours' notice so the slot can go to another student. Late cancellations may be charged.
